Voiced vs. Voiceless Sounds (Vocal Cords)

This is the most crucial mechanical split in English speech. Voiced sounds require your vocal cords to physically vibrate, whereas Voiceless sounds are made with pure air passing through your teeth and lips.

Voiceless PURE AIR

Touch your throat while pronouncing these sounds. You will feel zero mechanical vibration:

/p/ (pen) /s/ (see) /f/ (fan) /ʃ/ (she)

Rhythm & Structure: Word Stress

In English, words can change their meaning entirely based on which syllable is physically stressed. Observe this noun versus verb difference:

NOUN - Stress on 1st syllable REcord A physical disk or document of information.
VERB - Stress on 2nd syllable reCORD To save audio, video, or data onto a device.
